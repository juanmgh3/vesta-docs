## Phase 0 — interview the founder FIRST (inputs research cannot invent)

The business is deliberately not fully defined yet (founder, 2026-07-07:
service area undecided). Before searching anything, ask Juan, one at a time:

1. Service area to research (Granada capital? province? another city?).
2. Who performs the service and the money model (one-off service, packs,
   subscription?) — even a provisional answer scopes competitor comparison.
3. His relationship with Hyla (distributor / partner / just the equipment) —
   this changes what may legally/commercially be claimed on the page.
4. Price posture (premium vs accessible) — anchors the competitor pricing
   table.

If an answer is "not decided", record it as UNDECIDED and research the
options space instead of assuming one.

## Research questions (each answered with sources, in Spanish-market terms)

1. **Demand language.** What do 45–65 Spaniards actually type/say about this
   problem: "limpieza de colchones a domicilio", "ácaros", "alergia",
   "chinches" (adjacent!), "limpieza de sofás/tapicerías". Which frames
   dominate: health/allergy vs. cleanliness/stains vs. odor? Evidence:
   search-suggest data, foros (forocoches/enfemenino level included),
   competitor ad copy. Deliver: a vocabulary table (their words, not ours).
2. **Competitor map.** National players (franchises, e.g. mattress-cleaning
   chains), local cleaners who list upholstery services, and the DIY
   substitute (rented machines, home remedies). For each: offer, visible
   prices, claims they make, what their landing pages emphasize, reviews
   (what customers praise/complain about — complaint patterns are copy
   gold). Deliver: table + 5 screenshots-worth of claim language.
3. **Customer pains ranked.** Dust-mite allergy (prevalence in Spain,
   pediatric angle), asthma, skin conditions, "mattress never washed in 15
   years" shame, post-illness/incontinence events, pets. Which pains have
   MONEY behind them (people already paying)? Evidence: review mining,
   health-org data (SEAIC etc.), competitor positioning.
4. **Claims & legal guardrails.** What health/efficacy claims are safe in
   Spanish advertising for this service ("elimina el 99% de ácaros" needs
   substantiation — find what competitors get away with vs. what consumer
   law/autocontrol actually permits). Deliver: a "claims we can/cannot
   make" list — this constrains `copy.ts` more than anything else.
5. **Paid-traffic reality.** Google Ads landscape for these keywords in
   Spain: who advertises, ad copy patterns, rough CPC ranges IF a credible
   source exists (label estimates as estimates — no invented numbers).
6. **Seasonality & triggers.** When does demand spike (spring/allergy
   season? post-summer? moving house?) — informs launch timing and ad
   scheduling.

## Method standard (non-negotiable)

- Multi-modal sweep: search by problem, by competitor name, by symptom, by
  region. One angle never finds everything.
- Every claim in the report carries a source; confidence labeled as
  FACT (sourced) / ESTIMATE (derived, show the derivation) / HYPOTHESIS
  (unverified — needs founder validation or a test).
- Adversarially verify the load-bearing claims (the ones copy would be
  built on) with a second independent source before they graduate to FACT.
- Spanish-language sources take priority; the audience is not on
  English-language review sites.

## Deliverable

One report in `docs/research/` — manual, not summary: vocabulary table,
competitor table, ranked pains with evidence, claims guardrail list,
recommended positioning options (2–3, with trade-offs, founder decides).
Everything the copy pass will need to NOT invent.
