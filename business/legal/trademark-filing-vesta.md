## 1. Decisions taken (and why)

| Field                | Value                                              | Why                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Office               | OEPM (national mark, Spain)                        | Business operates in Spain only; an EUTM costs 6× more and widens the conflict map.                                      |
| Applicant (titular)  | Nelson Gil, natural person (autónomo)              | The applicant must be a legal person. "Grupo Vesta" does not exist legally yet; once an S.L. exists, assign the mark to it (`cesión`, a cheap recordal). |
| Type of mark         | Figurativa (logo + word "VESTA")                   | A word-only VESTA would sit on top of M4059079 (VESTA, class 37). The column-V logo adds a distinctive element.           |
| Image to file        | `assets/vesta-marca-figurativa-bn.jpg` (black)     | Black-and-white is the usual choice; colour claims narrow protection. Keep the teal JPG as a fallback.                   |
| Class                | 37 only                                            | Cleaning, disinfection and pest control all live in 37. Class 35 collides with three live VESTA marks and is not needed unless products are sold under the brand. |
| Service list         | §2 (harmonised terms + two free-text lines)        | Narrow and honest: no "construcción", "reparación" or "mantenimiento de edificios", which is what the prior VESTA marks cover. |
| Prior consent        | Letter to Vesta Rehabilitación, S.L. (§4)          | They opposed VESTA GROUP in April 2026; a signed consent before filing removes the opposition risk.                      |

Address question: the form takes the applicant's real domicile (Murcia). It is used for
official notifications and is public in the CEO file, nothing more. No client reads a
trademark's domicile to decide whether to hire. The Madrid-vs-Murcia question belongs to
the company's registered seat (`domicilio social`), which by law must be where the company
is actually managed, so it is not solved here either.

## 2. Service list for class 37 (paste into "Productos y servicios")

All lines except the last two are harmonised terms (HDB, accepted by OEPM and EUIPO; read
on TMclass on 2026-10-19). The last two are plain-language free text; the examiner may ask
to reword them but they are clear and belong in class 37.

Carpets are deliberately left out (founder decision 2026-10-19: no carpet machinery yet).
A mark cannot be widened after filing, only narrowed, so adding "limpieza de alfombras"
later means a new application. The harmonised terms for that day are "Limpieza de
alfombras" and "Desodorización de alfombras".

```
Limpieza de tapicerías; desinfección de tapizados; limpieza de muebles; limpieza de
interiores de edificios; limpieza de edificios [interiores]; desinfección; desinfección de
superficies interiores de edificios;
desinfección de superficies interiores domésticas para reducir la propagación de virus;
servicios de exterminación y control de plagas, distintos de los que sirven para uso
agrícola, acuícola, hortícola y forestal; limpieza y desinfección de colchones, somieres,
almohadas, sofás y textiles del hogar; eliminación de ácaros y alérgenos en colchones,
tapicerías y textiles del hogar.
```

## 5. Filing walkthrough (sede.oepm.gob.es)

What Nelson needs at hand: Cl@ve or digital certificate, DNI, real address, the JPG from
`assets/`, the service list from §2, a card for 127,88 €.

1. https://sede.oepm.gob.es → Marcas → "Solicitud de marcas, nombres comerciales y
   transformaciones" → marca nacional, nueva solicitud. Identify with Cl@ve/certificate.
2. Solicitante: persona física, Nelson Gil, NIF, domicilio real, e-mail (notifications are
   electronic; use an inbox someone reads). No representative.
3. Tipo de marca: Figurativa. Denominación: VESTA. Upload the JPG. Do not claim colour
   (`reivindicación de color`: no) when filing the black version.
4. Clases: 37. Paste §2 into the free-text list (the form also offers a term picker; the
   harmonised lines will match, the last two go in as free text).
5. No priority claim, no collective/guarantee mark, no accelerated procedure needed.
6. Review the summary, pay by card, download the `justificante` with the application number
   (M-number). Save it in `business/legal/`.
7. Diary: BOPI publication (~4 weeks), opposition window (2 months after), any `suspenso`
   from the examiner must be answered within the stated deadline. Check the file at
   https://ceo.oepm.es/detalleExpediente?numExp=<M-number>.

The exact labels on the sede form may differ from the wording above; the walkthrough is
done live in the browser, with Nelson entering credentials and payment himself.
