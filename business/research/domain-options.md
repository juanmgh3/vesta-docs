# Domain options for Vesta — names and registrars

Research note, 2026-10-27. Supersedes the three finalists of 2026-10-06 in
`business/visibility/plan-visibilidad.html` (vestahogar.es, vestahigiene.es, vestaencasa.es).
Labels: FACT = read on the registry, registrar or site that day, with source. SECONDARY =
comparison site or memory. ASSUMPTION = inferred, not read.

## 0. Outcome

Decision taken 2026-10-27: vestalimpia.es + vestalimpia.com at DonDominio (`business/visibility/decisions.md`).

- Recommended: **vestalimpia.es**, with **vestalimpia.com** bought alongside it (both free,
  FACT, two sources). Short, Spanish, dictates well by phone, reads as a sentence in an ad
  ("Vesta limpia tu sofá"), covers mattresses and sofas alike, does not tie the brand to
  Murcia, and adds a descriptor to "Vesta", which is already a registered word mark in class 37
  (`trademark-search-vesta.md`).
- Runner-up: vestalimpieza.es (.com is a cleaning company in Quito). Third: vestaencasa.es
  (long for ad headlines; .com is an Italian ceramics shop).
- Dropped: **vestahogar.es**, the 2026-10-06 favourite, because vestahogar.com is a
  financial spam/scam page ("transfer money with only card number and CVV", FACT, visited
  2026-10-27). A customer who types .com by habit lands there.
- Registrar: **DonDominio** (Spanish company, flat €6.95 + IVA = €8.41 for .es every year,
  free inbound transfer) or OVH (€7.25 first year, €8.46 renewal, IVA included). Equal on
  price; DonDominio invoices from Spain. Avoid Nominalia (renewal ≈ €78/yr), GoDaddy and
  Hostinger (hidden 2–3 year minimums).

## 3. About .es itself

- Registrant privacy: OVH's page says Whois.es shows only the holder's name and protects
  personal data (FACT, registrar page); IONOS guide says Red.es shows personal data as
  "no divulgados" since GDPR (SECONDARY). No explicit 2026 policy found on dominios.es.
- Periods 1–10 years (FACT, OVH and Dinahosting). Admin, technical and billing contacts
  must be natural persons (FACT, OVH and Dinahosting).
- Transfer: the new registrar files the request and the domain's administrative contact
  approves it (FACT, dominios.es/es/gestiona-tu-dominio/cambia-de-agente-registrador). No
  auth code is mentioned; no ICANN-style 60-day lock (ASSUMPTION).
- After expiry the domain is deactivated; Dinahosting mentions a 10-day grace period,
  dominios.es FAQ gives 5 extra days to pay when managed directly.
- The holder must be the socio fundador (owner in law), as `plan-visibilidad.html` already says.
