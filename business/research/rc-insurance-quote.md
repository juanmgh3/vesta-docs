## Conclusion (for the economic model)

**ESTIMATE: €25/month (≈€300/year), capital asegurado €150,000.** Booked as a fixed
cost in all scenarios. Not legally mandatory for this activity (FACT — not a regulated
profession with compulsory RC), but strongly advisable and expected by a bank.

- Typical coverage tiers offered: €150k / €300k / €600k; for a solo operator working
  inside private homes (material-damage risk), €150k–300k is the standard tier.
- No source publishes a price for "limpieza de tapicerías a domicilio" specifically;
  closest priced category = autónomo/empresa de limpieza (same in-home damage risk).
- Justification of €300/yr: mid-point between generalist entry prices (€99–120) and
  the small-cleaning-company example (€500 at 3 workers); defensible without
  overstating fixed costs.
