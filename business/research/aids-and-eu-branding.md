Reading (ANALYSIS): the footer "stickers" are not a badge one chooses to add. They are an
obligation that comes with any NextGenerationEU-funded aid, and Nelson already holds one.
As a Kit Digital beneficiary he is entitled to show them, and for the subsidized solution
he is expected to. The set is exactly three items: EU emblem, the sentence "Financiado por
la Unión Europea - NextGenerationEU", and the Plan de Recuperación logo. The Kit Digital
logo is optional alongside them. Cuota Cero and the SEF lines are state- or region-funded
and bring no EU logo.

  (€6,200 Madrid, €10,000 Galicia) are other regions' lines; Murcia's equivalents are §2
  and §3.
- INFO Murcia: no line for a solo service autónomo (carried from `murcia-market.md` §8.3).
- EU direct programmes: none found for a one-person local service; EU money reaches
  this profile only through Kit Digital (NextGenerationEU) or FSE+-cofinanced regional
  lines, and the two regional lines read here are not FSE+-funded.
- IRPF: new autónomos may apply a 7% retention on invoices to companies for the year of
  the alta and the two following. Not an aid, carried as context. SUPUESTO as to whether
  Nelson's gestor already applies it.
