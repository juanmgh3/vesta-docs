# Founder inputs — Phase 0 interview (Grupo Vesta business reports)

> Phase 0 deliverable of the business-report assignment (`docs/briefs/business-report.md`).
> Captured 2026-07-07 in a one-question-at-a-time founder interview. This file
> records every non-inventable input straight from the founder; nothing here is
> researched or assumed. Phase 1 (market research) runs in a separate session.

## How to read this file

Each item carries a status label:

- **CAPTURED** — a fact stated by the founder.
- **UNDECIDED** — no decision yet. Phase 1/2 researches the option space; never assume one.
- **FLAG-RESEARCH** — an explicit research task the founder asked for (Phase 1/2).
- **TO-CONFIRM** — a figure or validation still missing before it can be load-bearing.

Conventions in force (from the brief):

- **People by role only** — no names, no personal data anywhere in this file. The
  operator/founder is "el socio fundador"; the web/marketing role is "the web lead".
- **Currency** — Chile figures kept in CLP (source currency); EUR conversion is
  pending the exchange rate at report date, not fixed here.
- Web rules (motion, performance budget, design tokens) do **not** apply — this is
  a business-document track.

## Block 1 — The service

- **Catalogue.** Deep cleaning of textile upholstery — mattresses, sofas,
  upholstered furniture. This is the launch core (chosen partly because the machine
  needed for it is already on hand). CAPTURED.
- **Rugs.** Deprioritized — low margin and some are very hard to work. CAPTURED.
- **Cars.** Excluded from the launch catalogue. CAPTURED.
- **B2B** (hotels, short-term rentals, companies). Contemplated from the start but
  **subject to viability** — not guaranteed. Weight and timing to be determined by
  the reports. CAPTURED.
- **Service duration.** Not fixed — driven by soil level and piece size. Field
  planning estimate ≈ **1 h per service on average**. Range: ~15 min (small piece /
  light soiling) → king mattress ≈ 1.5 h → large or detailed sofa ≈ 1–1.5 h.
  CAPTURED (a range on purpose; a single number would be false precision and is the
  raw material for the conservative/realistic/optimistic scenarios).
- **Drying.** Natural / ambient, **seasonal** (summer dries much faster than
  winter). The operator **leaves** — does not wait on site — so drying does **not**
  limit daily capacity, only client experience. Future: fans / more professional
  drying equipment once revenue allows → possible service upsell. CAPTURED.
- **Daily capacity.** ≈ **4 services/day per operator** at launch (e.g. 4 mattresses
  or 4 large sofas). Single operator at start. CAPTURED.

## Block 2 — Own numbers

- **Hyla relationship.** Hyla is the **machine supplier** — not a distributor /
  commission model. CAPTURED.
- **Equipment (day 1).** Already owned and complete: **1 Hyla vacuum + accessories +
  drill** used in the process. Nothing to buy to start; operationally ready. One
  machine covers one operator (not a multi-machine kit). CAPTURED.
- **Equipment cost.** Complete pack ≈ **€2,300**, one-off, **already paid**. CAPTURED
  (sunk capital; amortize in the model; this figure sets the cost of a second machine
  when scaling to a second operator).
- **Variable cost per service.** Consumables (brush, products) ≈ **€1.5/service**.
  **Water and electricity are provided by the client** (work is done in their home)
  → not a Vesta cost. CAPTURED. Direct cost per service is essentially operator time
  plus ~€1.5 in consumables plus travel (still TBD); the margin ceiling is set by
  operator-hours and travel, not by production cost.
- **Travel / desplazamiento.** **UNDECIDED** — varies with distance.
  **FLAG-RESEARCH:** how best to cover it (fold into price? charge separately? by
  zone/radius?). The founder explicitly asked for help here. Ties to Block 4
  (city/radius) — same decision seen from two sides.
- **Legal form.** **Autónomo** at start; the _alta_ is already done. CAPTURED.
- **Autónomo monthly quota (cuota).** TO-CONFIRM / research — the _alta_ is done, but
  the recurring monthly cuota is a real fixed cost still to quantify (tarifa plana vs.
  general quota, depending on seniority of the alta).
- **Gestor (accountant).** €90/quarter (≈ **€30/month**). CAPTURED.
- **Insurance.** Car insurance only, **€50/month**. CAPTURED. Civil-liability (RC)
  insurance for working inside client homes is **not** assumed — a consideration to
  validate in the Risks chapter, not a booked cost.
- **Vehicle.** Already owned; **no new vehicle** planned. The existing car is used for
  travel. CAPTURED.
- **Web running cost.** **FLAG-RESEARCH** — analyze hosting for a static, backend-less,
  non-SaaS-traffic site; a paid hosting server may not be cost-effective; find the best
  option. Preliminary orientation only (full analysis Phase 1): a static Astro site
  hosts at near-€0 on Pages-type platforms, so this cost tends toward zero.
- **Google Ads initial budget.** Intent: yes. Amount: **UNDECIDED** → the marketing
  report proposes a range.
- **Startup safety buffer.** **None reserved** → the model must treat **early
  break-even as a priority**, not a luxury.
- **Operation staffing.** The socio fundador operates **solo** (cleans + manages). The
  web lead handles web + web marketing only. **No payroll at launch.** CAPTURED.

## Block 3 — Price posture

- **Posture.** **UNDECIDED** — leaning toward adapting somewhat to market prices.
  Not decided; the architecture is a Phase 2 founder decision.
- **Strategic tension (captured, not resolved here — resolved in the Phase 2 pricing
  study + marketing report):**
  - _Founder:_ inclined to **low prices vs. competitors**, especially at start, to
    capture clients fast.
  - _Web lead:_ sees price-competition as potentially **counterproductive** — it can
    damage prestige, complicate future price increases, and self-relegate the brand to
    a "lower league".
  - The brief's Phase 2 already requires 2–3 price architectures with trade-offs,
    including **"match market price with a premium look"** — the middle ground between
    these two positions.
- **Chile reference (operating business, real prices a market already pays).**
  - Mattress, 2-plaza (double): **40,000 CLP**.
  - Mattress, 1-plaza (single): **28,000 CLP**.
  - FACT. Orientation ≈ €38–40 / €27–28 at ~1,000 CLP/€ — **exact conversion pending
    exchange rate at report date**, not fixed here. Use as an **independent
    cross-check anchor** for method, **not a target** (Chile purchasing power and
    competition differ from Spain; Spanish market prices are likely higher).

## Block 5 — Horizon

- **Projection span.** **8 years** — overrides the brief's default of 3. CAPTURED.
  - _Modeling implication (Phase 4):_ year 1 month-by-month, years 2–3 detailed, years
    4–8 as a **strategic trajectory** (annual + milestone-based), not false-precision
    monthly figures.
- **Strategic north star (impersonal).** The model should show a path from
  **"founder = sole operator"** to **"founder = owner / manager"** — a business that
  runs **without the founder performing the service personally** (hire operators, bring
  in partners, delegate operations). The operating roadmap orients to that milestone:
  when to hire the first operator, when to delegate the operation.
- **Launch window.** **UNDECIDED** — operationally ready (equipment-wise), but the
  actual launch is gated by the relocation decision (Block 4).
