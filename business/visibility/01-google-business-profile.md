# 01 — Google Business Profile

First step of the visibility playbook. The profile is what shows on Google Maps and in the
local results, it is the location asset the Google Ads campaign will use, Bing Places imports
it, and it is one of the sources AI assistants read when asked for a local service. It needs
no domain and no prices, so it starts now.

It goes first because it is the slowest step: video verification is reviewed in up to 5
business days (FACT, Google help page, read 2026-10-12).

Labels: FACT = read or measured, with source and date. ASSUMPTION = believed, not checked;
the line says what checks it.

## Ownership

- One Google account for the business, not a personal one: a new Gmail with 2-step
  verification and the business phone as recovery. The same account will later own Google
  Ads, Search Console and Analytics, so everything lives under one identity.
- The web lead operates it. The socio fundador is added as a second owner of the profile
  once it exists, so the business never depends on one person's login.

## Steps

1. Create the business Google account.
2. Go to business.google.com and add a business: name, primary category, answer **No** to
   "add a location customers can visit", add the service areas, the phone, skip the website,
   and enter the verification address (the socio fundador's address in Murcia; hidden).
3. Record the verification video when the profile asks for it (rules below).
4. While the review runs, complete the profile: description, services, hours and photos (copy
   below). Photos must be real: logo, cover, and at least five of the machine, the work in
   progress and real before/after. No client faces, no stock images.
5. After verification: copy the "Pedir reseñas" link into the WhatsApp template below,
   publish a first post, and leave the profile ready to link to Google Ads and Bing Places.

## Verification video

FACT, Google help page (support.google.com/business/answer/14271705, Spanish version, read
2026-10-12): recorded live from a phone inside the Business Profile, one continuous take with
no edits or pauses, at least 30 seconds. A service-area business shows three things, where it
operates, that it exists, and that the person filming manages it. No other people's faces and
no sensitive data (IDs, tax numbers, bank details).

Script, one take, about 45 seconds:

1. Outside, near the verification address: the street name sign and the building number,
   then a recognisable landmark (where it operates).
2. Walk to the car and open the boot: the HYLA machine, accessories, drill and products (it
   exists).
3. Switch the HYLA on and vacuum a mattress or sofa for a few seconds (manages it).
4. If there is anything branded "Vesta" (card, polo, sign), show it; the name must match the
   profile exactly.

Do not use the alta or other documents as proof: they carry the socio fundador's legal name and
tax number, not "Vesta", so they show a mismatch and sensitive data at once. If the video is
rejected, the new one must include everything in the first plus what was missing.

## Reviews

- Only from real customers. Fake or AI-written reviews are illegal in Spain (RDL 24/2021, see
  `business/research/marketing-research-addendum.md` R4) and Google removes them.
- Ask every customer, the same way. Asking only the happy ones, or steering unhappy ones
  elsewhere first ("review gating"), is against Google's policy, as is offering a discount for
  a review (ASSUMPTION: checked against current policy before the first request goes out).
- Reply to every review, good or bad, within two days.
