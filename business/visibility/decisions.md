# Visibility decisions

Dated record of the decisions that shape what Vesta says in public (website, Google Business
Profile, ads, directories). Entries are never deleted; a reversed decision is marked
superseded, with its cause, and points to the new one.

## 2026-10-14 — names, claims and what stays unsaid (web lead)

- **Names.** "Vesta" is the public name everywhere (website, profile, ads, WhatsApp).
  "Grupo Vesta" is the trade name used on the legal side (legal notice, invoices); the
  legal holder is the socio fundador as autónomo.
- **Prices.** No public price list; every job is quoted by WhatsApp. A multi-piece discount
  may be published without prices ("a partir de dos piezas, un 10 % menos"), pending the
  socio fundador's OK on the discount itself.
- **Not said in public:** response hours (the socio fundador answers at any time), drying
  time and how damp the fabric stays (depends on the service), what Vesta does not do, and
  any guarantee. Removing a claim is always allowed; the current website copy that states
  these ("en seco", "sin días de secado", "precios publicados") goes.
- **What the service removes:** dust, dust mites and their debris, pet hair and odours,
  never as an absolute ("elimina el 100 %" and lab-style percentages are out: there is no
  test behind them). The message leans on what cannot be seen.
- **Internal only, still needed:** what Vesta does not do feeds the Google Ads negative
  keyword list (car upholstery, for one) and the WhatsApp quick replies.
- **Products.** Public copy may say the products are approved only once the socio fundador
  names them; no seal, badge or certification is shown unless Vesta actually holds it.

Pending with the socio fundador: internal tariff and minimum order, multi-piece discount,
service area and out-of-area surcharge, Google Ads test cap, product names, recommended
repeat interval, payment methods.

## 2026-10-14 — the socio fundador's answers (resolves the pending list above, except where noted)

- **Internal tariff, IVA included, not published.** Colchón individual €55, matrimonio €69,
  king €89; sofá 2 plazas €79, 3 plazas €95, chaise longue or rinconera €110; sillón €40;
  silla €10; cabecero not set yet. The socio fundador checked it against Murcia prices
  himself and found it in line.
- **Minimum per visit:** €55, the price of a single mattress, so no mattress or sofa job is
  turned away; it only tops up visits for loose small pieces.
- **Multi-piece discount:** −10 % on the cheapest piece from two pieces per visit. Can be
  published without prices.
- **Service area without surcharge:** Murcia and pedanías, Molina de Segura, Alcantarilla,
  Las Torres de Cotillas, Santomera, Beniel, Alguazas, Ceutí, Lorquí, Archena. Outside it, a
  surcharge that covers the trip (Orihuela or Cieza +€10; Cartagena, Torrevieja or Mar Menor
  +€20; Lorca +€30), or jobs grouped on one day. Trip costs from `murcia-market.md` §8.5.
- **Google Ads test:** €250 in total over 6–8 weeks, set as a campaign total budget so the
  campaign cannot bill beyond it; spend is charged as it accrues, not upfront.
- **Products:** FREM (Grupo Industrial Falon), used in Granada; here an upholstery shampoo
  and a degreaser, brand still to confirm. The ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certificates are the
  manufacturer's, not Vesta's: copy may say the products come from a certified manufacturer,
  and shows no certification seal or FREM logo without FREM's permission. "Homologados" is
  not used.
- **Repeat interval:** once a year for an adult couple; two or three times a year with
  children or teenagers. Feeds the reminders and an FAQ answer.
- **Payment methods:** cash, Bizum, bank transfer and card. Card is listed only once a card
  reader exists (pending).

## 2026-10-27 — domain (web lead)

- **vestalimpia.es** is the domain; **vestalimpia.com** is bought with it and redirects.
  Chosen over vestalimpieza.es (the "Vesta + limpieza" formula is already used by a cleaning
  company in Quito that holds the .com and by a "Vesta Limpieza Integral" on Instagram) and
  over vestahogar.es, the 2026-10-06 favourite (vestahogar.com is a financial scam page).
  Evidence and prices in `business/research/domain-options.md`.
- Registrar: DonDominio (Spanish, flat €6.95 + IVA per .es, €15.95 + IVA per .com, pays by
  card, PayPal or Bizum). Holder of both domains: the socio fundador. The web lead operates
  the account and points the DNS at Cloudflare.
- Optional, not decided: vestalimpieza.es as a typo redirect (+€6.95 + IVA a year).
